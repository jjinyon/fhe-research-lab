## 🇺🇸 English

### 📌 About

`fhe-research-lab` is a personal research project for studying, implementing, and exploring research ideas in **Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE)**.

The project currently focuses on **OpenFHE and CKKS**, with the goal of understanding the fundamental challenges of FHE through theory and experiments.

### 🎯 Research Areas

* Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE)
* CKKS
* Noise & Approximation
* Multiplicative Depth
* Bootstrapping
* Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning
* Homomorphic Neural Network Inference
* Interactive FHE

### 🧪 Research Process

```text
Theory
  ↓
Implementation
  ↓
Experiment
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Research Question
  ↓
Research Idea
```

The goal is to go beyond implementation and discover new research questions from experimental observations.
